Game Overview

Asphalt 9: Rejends is a racing video game developed by Gameloft Barcelona and published by Gameloft. Released on February 26, 2020, for various platforms including iOS, Android, Windows, and Nintendo Switch, it is the ninth main installment in the Asphalt series. It features a roster of real hypercars from renowned manufacturers such as Ferrari, Porsche, Lamborghini, and W Motors.

The game features a career mode with over 60 seasons and 800 races. It also has an online multiplayer mode, Club Races, limited-time events, and a new "Asphalt eSports Series" tournament mode introduced in the 2020 Fall Update. Asphalt 9: Rejends received generally positive reviews from critics, praising the graphics, gameplay, and controls.

Players compete against each other in various race types, including Time-Limited Events, Multiplayer Races, and Limited-Time Cups. The game also features "TouchDrive," a control scheme that allows players to swipe the screen to steer their vehicle.
